CartSense Explainable Recommendations

ProblemSmall online stores show the same bestsellers to everyone. Most visitors never sign in, black-box suggestions aren't trusted, and there's no easy way to prove recommendations help.

SolutionAn end-to-end, explainable recommendation system: an ML pipeline trained on 814K real Amazon reviews, a versioned API that any store can call, and a working storefront that proves its impact with live A/B tests.

0real reviews modelled
0NDCG@10 vs best baseline
0ranking features engineered
  • Two-stage hybrid: ALS, item-kNN and TF-IDF candidates re-ranked by XGBoost LambdaMART (24 features), +39% NDCG@10 over the best single model
  • Explainable and login-free: every pick answers "Why this?", and guests get personal recommendations after one product view
  • Proves its impact: a built-in A/B test against a popularity baseline, with CTR and add-to-bag rate checked by a two-proportion z-test
  • Production-style: leakage-free evaluation in MLflow, retraining on live store events, and a versioned FastAPI with API keys for other stores

VIT Bhopal Sports Facilities

Problem1,000+ students share a few pickleball, tennis and turf courts, with no fair way to book slots, verify players at the gate or reclaim no-shows.

SolutionA production booking and access-control platform live on campus. Students book with their college account, guards verify a QR pass at the gate, and the sports office manages courts, policies and analytics from one dashboard, with every rule enforced in the database.

0students on the platform
0automated tests
0tables under Row-Level Security
  • Concurrency-safe transactions: partial unique indexes, per-slot advisory locks and idempotency keys keep bookings consistent on unreliable mobile networks
  • Security and RBAC: Row-Level Security on all 16 tables, SECURITY DEFINER functions, college-only Google OAuth/OTP, ES256 JWT verification, Zod validation, rate limiting and a strict CSP
  • Automated operations: QR check-in, waitlist offers, no-show release and weekly turf scheduling run as idempotent pg_cron jobs, with health monitoring
  • Tested and deployed: 239 automated tests (Vitest + PGlite) that probe RLS as every role; TypeScript monorepo on Vercel and Render
